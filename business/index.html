Questions

Business Order FAQ

Is there a minimum order?

No — we work with orders of any size, from a single piece to large bulk runs. Pricing simply scales more efficiently at higher quantities.

What file type should I send?

Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG or high-res PDF) give the cleanest result. A high-resolution PNG or JPG works too — if your logo needs cleanup or digitizing for embroidery, we can handle that.

Can I mix garment sizes and colors?

Yes. Tell us the size and color breakdown for your team when you request your quote and we'll build it into your order.

How long does production take?

Most business orders are ready within 1–2 weeks after you approve your digital proof, depending on order size and product mix.

Do I approve the design first?

Always. You'll receive a digital proof of your logo placement and branding before we begin production.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes — we offer local pickup in Newport News, VA, and nationwide shipping for businesses anywhere in the U.S.

Can I reorder the same design later?

Yes — we keep your logo setup and branding specs on file, so reordering for new hires or additional team members is quick.
