Choosing the Right Sign for Your Business: Materials, Sizes & Placement

V Imprint DesignsNewport News, VA~5 min read

A sign is one of those things you order once and live with for years, so it's worth getting right. Here's a practical walkthrough of the decisions that actually matter — material, size, and where it's going to live.

Define the job of the sign

Start with what the sign needs to do. Is it a storefront sign meant to be seen from the street? An interior lobby sign meant to reinforce your brand up close? A directional or door sign meant to be functional first? The job changes the size, material, and even the lettering style that makes sense.

Material options

  • Acrylic — clean, modern, works well indoors or under cover
  • Wood — warm and classic, great for boutique or rustic-brand spaces
  • Metal — the most durable option for full outdoor exposure
  • Vinyl & printed graphics — affordable for windows and temporary signage

Size & placement

A sign that's too small gets lost; one that's too large can overwhelm a small storefront. As a rough guide, lettering should be readable from the distance your typical customer will be standing or driving — a few inches tall for a lobby sign, much larger for anything meant to be seen from the road. If you're not sure, send us a photo of the space and we'll recommend sizing.

Indoor vs. outdoor durability

Indoor signs have more flexibility — wood and acrylic both hold up well without direct weather exposure. For anything outdoors, metal or weather-rated acrylic is the safer choice, since materials like untreated wood can warp or fade with sun and rain over time.

Budget & timeline

Sign pricing depends mostly on size, material, and complexity of the design — a simple acrylic logo sign costs less than a large dimensional metal sign with lighting. We'll give you a clear quote once we know the size and material, along with a production timeline.

Common questions

Choosing a business sign: FAQ

What material should I choose for an outdoor sign?

Metal is the most durable choice for full outdoor exposure. Weather-rated acrylic can also work well depending on the design.

How do I know what size sign I need?

Send us a photo of the space and we'll recommend sizing based on viewing distance and where it'll be placed.

Is acrylic or wood better for a lobby sign?

Both work well indoors — acrylic gives a cleaner, modern look, while wood suits a warmer, more boutique brand feel.

Can you add lighting to a sign?

Yes — backlit and edge-lit options are available for signs that need to stand out in lower light.

How much does a custom sign cost?

It depends on size, material, and complexity. Send us your logo and a photo of the space and we'll give you a clear, free quote.

How long does a sign take to produce?

Turnaround depends on the size and material — we'll give you a clear timeline along with your quote.
