QR Code Signs and Metal Business Cards: Modern Ways to Get Noticed Locally

V Imprint DesignsNewport News, VA~4 min read

A little bit of modern polish goes a long way. QR code signage and metal business cards are two small touches that make a business feel current — and they both do real work, not just look nice.

Why modern touches get remembered

People are used to scanning things — menus, payment links, reviews. A sign or card that makes it effortless to find you online stands out simply because it removes friction. Instead of asking someone to type your website into their phone, a QR code gets them there in one tap.

QR code signs & displays

We can add a QR code to almost any sign — a storefront sign linking to your menu or booking page, a table tent linking to reviews, or a lobby sign linking to your portfolio. It's a small design element that makes your signage do more work for you, without changing the look.

Brushed metal business cards

A metal business card is memorable in a way paper rarely is — it has weight, it looks premium, and people tend to hold onto it instead of tossing it. For a business that relies on referrals and word-of-mouth, that's a small edge worth having.

Combining them for impact

A metal card with a QR code, paired with matching QR signage at your location, creates a consistent, modern touch across every place a customer interacts with your brand — in hand and in person.

How to order

Tell us what the QR code should link to — your website, booking page, menu, or reviews — and we'll build it into your sign or card design. We'll show you a proof before anything goes into production.

Common questions

QR signs & metal cards: FAQ

Can you add a QR code to any sign?

Yes — we can add a QR code to almost any sign design, linking to your website, menu, booking page, or reviews.

What are metal business cards made of?

Brushed metal cards, giving them a premium weight and finish compared to standard paper cards.

Do QR codes expire?

The physical code itself doesn't expire, but it's smart to link it to a page you control so you can update where it points later if needed.

Can I combine a QR code with my logo?

Yes — we design the code and your branding together so it fits naturally into the sign or card.

Are metal cards more expensive than paper cards?

They cost more per card than standard paper, but many businesses find the impression and retention worth it for a smaller, targeted batch.

How do I get started?

Tell us what you'd like the QR code to link to and whether you're interested in signage, cards, or both, and we'll put together a quote.
